## ⚙️ AI Setup

NØTUNE is "Free-First". You can use it as a powerful music player without any AI configuration. To unlock intelligence:

1. Obtain a free API key from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/) or [Groq](https://groq.com/).
2. Navigate to **Settings > AI Settings**.
3. Enter your key and test the connection.
4. Enable **AI DJ** or **Experimental AI** in the NØTUNE Lab.

## 🛡️ Privacy & Security

- **Local First**: Your music library and listening history remain on your device.
- **No Data Harvesting**: We do not track or sell your personal information.
- **Secure Credentials**: API keys are stored encrypted in Android DataStore and never transmitted to our servers.
- **Open Source**: The code is transparent and auditable by the community.
