
# Changelog

All notable changes to **NØTUNE** will be documented in this file.

## [1.2.6] - 2026-02-18
### Added
- **NØTUNE Identity**: Complete rebranding from Echo Music to NØTUNE.
- **NØTUNE AI Engine**: Multi-provider AI support (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama).
- **AI DJ**: Intelligent, futuristic song introductions based on Music DNA.
- **Ask NØTUNE**: Natural language music assistant with tool-calling capabilities.
- **Theme Builder**: Extreme customization of blur, glass, accent colors, and typography.
- **Neural Map**: Geometric visualization of user's musical influences.
- **Replay**: Monthly listening statistics and top tracks report.
- **Spectrum Visualizer**: Real-time audio bars on the immersive player.
- **Industrial Onboarding**: New technical initialization process for first-time users.

### Changed
- **UI Design**: Shifted to a sharp, minimalist "Nothing OS" industrial aesthetic.
- **Geometry**: Defaulted to 2dp sharp corners for a technical feel.
- **Streaming**: Enhanced client-cascade with 12+ fallback clients for better reliability.

### Fixed
- Improved background playback stability.
- Resolved various 403 errors during YouTube Music streaming.
- Corrected metadata parsing for local media files.
